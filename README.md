📊 Amazon AI-Powered Business Intelligence Dashboard
🚀 Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution built using MySQL, Power BI, SQL, and Data Analytics techniques. The objective was to transform raw Amazon-style transaction and customer data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards and analytical reporting.

The project covers:

Sales Performance Analysis
Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis)
Product Performance Tracking
Geographic Revenue Analysis
Customer Sentiment Analysis
Executive Business KPIs

The solution enables business stakeholders to monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

🎯 Business Problem

Large e-commerce businesses generate massive amounts of transaction, customer, and review data.

Key challenges include:

Understanding customer purchasing behavior
Identifying top-performing products
Tracking revenue across regions
Measuring customer loyalty and retention
Monitoring customer sentiment
Generating actionable business insights

This project addresses these challenges through an integrated Business Intelligence dashboard.
